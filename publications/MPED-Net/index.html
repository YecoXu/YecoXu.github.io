Planar-Constrained Wireframe Generation for Building Roof Reconstruction from Airborne LiDAR Point Clouds

Xu YiKe1 Cao Li1 Liu XiaoPing1

1School of Computer and Information Science, Hefei University of Technology

Abstract

In the field of photogrammetry, the precise modeling of roof structures from airborne LiDAR point clouds is still considered a formidable task. This challenge is primarily attributed to the inherent geometric complexity of roofs, exacerbated by sensor noise, non-uniform sampling, and feature blurring in real-world data. Mainstream corner-based methodologies often suffer from localized detection errors, leading to fragmented or topologically inconsistent reconstructions. Moreover, the sparsity of edge samples in LiDAR data has hindered the effective utilization of sharp boundaries as structural constraints. To address these limitations, an automated roof reconstruction framework based on planar-constrained wireframe generation is presented. First, a multi-task deep learning architecture, MPED-Net, is constructed to synergistically perform planar segmentation, surface normal estimation, and edge denoising. This stage provides high-fidelity geometric primitives by effectively suppressing sampling artifacts. Subsequently, a reconstruction pipeline guided by geometric priors is developed, where robust 3D wireframes are extracted via ray-projection under planar constraints. To finalize the surface, a constrained Delaunay triangulation is executed, enhanced by a novel centroid filtering criterion to enforce non-convex topological consistency. The superior performance of the proposed method is validated through extensive experiments on the Building3D dataset, demonstrating its capability to achieve high-fidelity reconstruction from noisy airborne LiDAR point clouds. Finally, the experimental dataset is made publicly available to facilitate further research in urban reconstruction.
